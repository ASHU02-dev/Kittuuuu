PAGE II · HOW WE EVEN GOT HERE

Pehle toh tu accept kar leti request.

Technically iss story ka thoda credit Kashish ko bhi jaata hai. Uski wajah se hum dono ko ek dusre ko jaanne ka chance mila.

Aur phir hum actually baat karne lage, ek dusre ko samajhne lage... aur pata hi nahi chala kab conversations thodi zyada important hone lagi.

But then there was one tiny problem...

"Madam ne meri request accept karne mein itne mahine laga diye ki mujhe laga tha Instagram ka server issue hai." 😂

Tu waise bhi sabke saath thodi mature, reserved aur avoidant type hai. Toh obviously mujhe bhi special treatment nahi milna tha. 😂

But honestly, ab peeche dekh ke funny lagta hai. Kyunki agar us time mujhe pata hota ki months wait karne ke baad tu itni important ban jayegi, toh shayad main aur patience se wait karta.

One last thing I wanted to give you...
22 August 2026
To My Dear Girl

Kritika,

I don't really know how to put everything I feel for you into words. Shayad isi liye ye letter itna late aa raha hai. Kuch feelings aisi hoti hain jinko likhne ke liye sirf words enough nahi lagte.

But today, on your birthday, I want to say it without overthinking it.

I love you. More than I probably know how to explain.

Aur ye sirf woh wala “I love you” nahi hai jo kisi moment mein bol diya aur phir khatam ho gaya. Ye woh feeling hai jo mere normal days ka part ban chuki hai.

Main kaam kar raha hota hoon, apne future ke baare mein soch raha hota hoon, kisi random jagah pe hota hoon... aur somehow thoughts tum tak aa hi jaate hain. Kabhi bina reason ke tum yaad aa jaati ho.

Kabhi tumhari koi baat yaad aati hai, kabhi tumhari voice, kabhi humari conversations, aur kabhi bas ye thought ki kaash tum iss waqt mere paas hoti.

Distance ne ek cheez toh sikha di hai — kisi ko miss karna sirf uske saath na hone ka naam nahi hai. It's carrying a person with you even when they're not physically there.

Aur main tumhe waise hi carry karta hoon. Mere thoughts mein, meri plans mein, mere future ke chhote-chhote ideas mein.

I miss you. A lot more than I usually tell you.

Mujhe pata hai abhi Shimla aur Bangalore ke beech ka distance kabhi kabhi unfair lagta hai. Kabhi patience test hota hai, kabhi loneliness feel hoti hai, aur kabhi mann karta hai ki bas sab kuch simple hota.

But I don't want us to be scared of that.

Mujhe yakeen hai ki hum phir milenge. Aur sirf milenge nahi — I want to build a life where we don't have to keep counting the distance between us.

Ek din main tumhe Bangalore le aaunga. Phir ye “Shimla se Bangalore” wala distance sirf ek old chapter hoga jo hum dono yaad karke smile karenge.

Aur haan, usmein thoda patience lagega. Things take time. Careers take time. Life takes time. Relationships take time.

But I don't mind waiting for something that I genuinely believe in.

I want us to become each other's strength. Jab tum tired ho, I want you to know I'm there. Jab main lose track karun, I want you to remind me why I'm doing all of this.

Main chahta hoon ki hum dono individually bhi grow karein. Tum apne dreams ke liye grow karo. Main apne dreams ke liye grow karun. Aur phir bhi, somewhere along the way, hum ek dusre ko choose karte rahein.

I want to watch you become the person you always wanted to be.

Aur main promise nahi karunga ki main hamesha perfect rahunga. I won't. Main bhi galtiyan karunga, kabhi stupid hounga, kabhi cheezein samajhne mein time lagaunga.

But I can promise you one thing — I'll keep trying to become better. For myself, for my future, and for the people I love.

Tumne mere birthday pe jo letter mujhe diya tha, uski value mere liye shayad tum realise bhi nahi karti. It meant a lot to me.

Aur honestly, mujhe thoda regret hai ki main tumhe physical letter nahi de paaya.

Isliye aaj ye digital letter sirf ek beginning hai. Ek din tumhe proper handwritten letter milega. Aisa letter jo main khud likhunga, pages pe pages.

Aur haan, agar zarurat padi toh blood se bhi likh dunga. Not because a letter needs blood, but because sometimes I want you to know how seriously I mean the things I say.

You deserve the best. Not someone who just says he loves you, but someone who works on himself enough to actually deserve you.

So I'll become that person.

Main perfect banne ka promise nahi karta. But I'll become the best version of myself that I possibly can.

The kind of man who can stand beside you, not above you. The kind of man who supports your growth, protects your peace, respects your dreams, and stays when things aren't easy.

Because I don't want to just be someone you love. I want to be someone you can rely on.

I want us to look back at this phase someday and say, “We actually made it through all of that.”

And when that day comes, I'll probably remind you that once upon a time you took months just to accept my request.

So yes, Cutu. Take your time. Grow. Dream. Become everything you're supposed to become.

I'll be doing the same.

And somewhere between all that growing, I'll still be right here.

Missing you. Thinking about you. Working for the future. And waiting for the day when I don't have to miss you from another city anymore.

Happy Birthday, Kritika.

Thank you for coming into my life in the most unexpected way possible.

And thank you for becoming someone I can genuinely say I love.

Until we meet again, keep a little faith in us.

Always yours,
The person who is still waiting
for that same girl who took months to accept him.
